Opt-out of Targeted Advertising

We and our third-party advertising partners use advertising identifiers (including Apple IDFA and Android Advertising ID) and tracking technologies to deliver personalized advertisements. You may disable personalized advertising through the following device settings:
iOS Devices:
Settings > Privacy > Advertising > Enable “Limit Ad Tracking” (iOS 13 and earlier); or Settings > Privacy > Tracking > Disable “Allow Apps to Request to Track” (iOS 14 and later).
Android Devices:
Settings > Google > Ads > Enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization”.
After opting out, you will only receive contextual non-personalized advertisements. Please note that you may still view generic advertisements, or personalized ads from third-party platforms obtained through other apps or websites. For detailed information, please refer to Appendix A: Advertising Networks and Partners.
To exercise your data rights, please submit a request via email: shorttailstudio@outlook.com. All legitimate user requests will be processed free of charge and responded to in a timely manner.
